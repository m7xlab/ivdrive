# Sponsor iVDrive

iVDrive is built and hosted independently. Sponsorships keep the project sustainable.

## Why sponsor

| Covers | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Hosting & domain** | Public beta at ivdrive.eu |
| **Backups & monitoring** | Keep your data safe |
| **Development** | Features, security, docs |

There is no corporate sponsor and no ads. If iVDrive helps your Škoda or VW Group EV, a small recurring contribution helps a lot.

## How to help

1. **GitHub Sponsors** — [github.com/sponsors/m7xlab](https://github.com/sponsors/m7xlab)
2. **Ko-fi** — [ko-fi.com/m7xlaboratory](https://ko-fi.com/m7xlaboratory)
3. **Product page** — [ivdrive.eu/sponsors](https://ivdrive.eu/sponsors/)

## Non-financial help

- Star the repo and share iVDrive with other EV owners  
- Report bugs and write docs  
- Open pull requests — see [Contributing](../community/contributing.md)

Thank you — every bit of support is noticed and appreciated.
