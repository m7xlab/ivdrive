# AI Support Coach

Ask questions about your vehicle data in plain language.

## What you can ask

Examples:

- “How much did I spend on charging last month?”
- “Why was my range worse this week?”
- “Show my battery health trend”
- “Compare home vs public charging costs”

The coach can return **answers, charts and next steps** based on your data.

## Enable

Available only where the deployment has AI features configured.

1. Open the coach widget in the app
2. Accept any privacy notice
3. Ask your first question

## Privacy & control

- Conversations are tied to your account
- Self-hosters can disable or bring their own keys (see configuration)
- You can clear conversation history where offered

## Tips

- Be specific about time ranges (“last 7 days”, “October”)
- Ask for a chart when you want a visual
- Use it alongside [Statistics](./statistics.md) — it helps you navigate to the right dashboard

:::note
The coach is an assistant, not a service advisor. For vehicle faults, contact Škoda service.
:::
