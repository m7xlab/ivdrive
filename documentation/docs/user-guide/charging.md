# Charging

See **when**, **where**, **how fast** and **how much** you charged — including cost.

## Charging on the vehicle page

The **Charging** panel shows live/s latest state:

- Charge state (Connected / Charging / Disconnected)
- Power (kW)
- Target SoC
- Time remaining (when charging)

## Charging sessions

Under **Statistics → Charging Stats**:

- Session history
- Energy delivered (kWh)
- Cost estimates
- AC / DC distribution
- Provider / location where available

## Charging curves

**Charging Curve** and **Curve Integrals** show power vs SoC over a session — useful to compare chargers and spot slow stations.

## Costs

iVDrive can estimate spend using:

- Your configured home / public rates
- Session energy × price

Configure plans in **Settings → Charging plans** (availability depends on deployment).

## Tips

1. Filter by AC vs DC when comparing speed.
2. Use curve charts to see where taper starts (e.g. after ~55% SoC).
3. Pair with [Battery health](./battery-health.md) to spot unusual heating or slowdowns.
